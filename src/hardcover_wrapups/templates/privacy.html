Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Hardcover Wrap-Ups makes an image of the covers of the books you finished in a month, from your Hardcover library. It is run by an individual, not a company, and isn't made by or affiliated with Hardcover. This page says what it reads, what it keeps, and who else sees any of it. The short version: it reads your books to draw one image, and stores nothing.

What it reads

When you sign in, Hardcover asks whether this site may read:

  • your profile basics: your Hardcover user ID and username;
  • the public and followers-only entries in your library.

It never asks for your email address, and it can't read library entries you've marked private.

When you make a wrap-up, it reads the books you finished in the month you picked: each book's title, authors, cover, and when you started and finished it. Followers-only books are included only if you tick the box for them.

What it keeps

Nothing about you or your books is stored on the server.

  • Your sign-in is kept only in your own browser, in an encrypted cookie that expires 15 minutes after you sign in. Signing out deletes it and asks Hardcover to cancel the sign-in. Hardcover also issues a longer-lived "refresh" token, which this site asks Hardcover to cancel immediately, unused.
  • The image is drawn in the server's memory, sent to your browser, and not saved anywhere. Downloading it is up to you.
  • Technical logs record each request's page address (without any query string), result and timing, so problems can be fixed. They don't include your IP address, your username, or your books. When something fails, the log records the error, which can include an error message from Hardcover or the web address of a cover that couldn't be loaded. Logs are kept by the host for a short time.

Cookies

The site uses two cookies, both needed for signing in: one that lasts 10 minutes while you sign in with Hardcover, and the encrypted sign-in cookie described above. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts.

Who else sees anything

  • Hardcover handles your sign-in and answers this site's requests for your books, under Hardcover's privacy policy.
  • Fly.io hosts the site in the United States. Like any host, its network handles your connection, including your IP address, under Fly.io's privacy policy.

Nobody else receives any of it. Your data is used only to make your image, as Hardcover's API terms require: it is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

There's nothing stored to delete. Signing out ends your sign-in right away, and it expires on its own after 15 minutes anyway. You can also remove Hardcover Wrap-Ups from the connected apps in your Hardcover account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
