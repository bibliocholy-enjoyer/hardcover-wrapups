Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Effective October 21, 2026

Privacy (the important stuff)

Hardcover Wrap-Ups was made by a nerdy individual, not a company, and is in no way affilited with Hardcover. The app needs access to the books on your Hardcover account to draw the image (not including your private books), but it doesn't store your books or images, and nothing is shared with third parties.

The site does keep anonymous daily totals, like how many people visited, created a wrap-up, or clicked download. To count unique users, a hashed version of your Hardcover username is created using a random salt that is discarded at the end of each month. IP addresses aren't recorded.

The site uses two encrypted cookies to keep you signed into Hardcover, and both are expired after fifteen minutes. There are no cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party scripts.

Terms (the legal stuff)

Hardcover Wrap-Ups is offered "as-is", with no guarantees that it will work correctly (or at all!). The operator isn't liable for any loss or harm that comes from using it or from not being able to use it.

This site doesn't host or publish images. If you own the copyright in a cover and want it left out of images this site makes, open an issue on GitHub naming the book and cover. Covers are from Hardcover, which has its own DMCA takedown policy.
